SkillPulse Outcome Methodology

Longitudinal Signal Tracking (3, 6, 12 Months)

Vocational skilling outcomes are assessed post-certification through proactive check-ins conducted at month 3, 6, and 12. These follow-ups verify employment continuity, real wage growth, and workplace safety.

Consent-First Civic Cartography

SkillPulse operates on strict participant consent. Learners retain full autonomy over their data and can grant or withdraw consent at any time. When consent is withdrawn, learner contact information is instantly purged from active follow-up queues and masked in administrative exports.

Aadhaar-Free Unified Identification

To prevent unnecessary data accumulation and safeguard participant privacy, SkillPulse uses pseudonymous Unified IDs (e.g. SP-24-0182). Raw Aadhaar numbers, biometric data, or sensitive government credentials are never stored.
